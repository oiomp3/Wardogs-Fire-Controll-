📖 How to use

How it works

  1. Your position: enter your weapon's X and Y – using the number field or the slider (0–160, 1 unit = 100 m).
  2. Target: enter the target's X and Y. ⇄ Swap exchanges position and target.
  3. Choose your weapon: L81 mortar (132–684 m) or SPH-2 artillery (~780–2629 m).
  4. Tap Calculate.

Reading the result

  • Distance in metres.
  • Compass / Azimuth in degrees with the compass direction, below it the azimuth in mils (6400 system).
  • Elevation (mils) – mortar only. For the SPH-2, please use community tables.
  • Green = in range, red = too close or out of range.

Copy & history

  • Copy result copies all values to the clipboard.
  • Recent shots keeps your last 15 calculations in the browser. Tap a shot to load it again, rename it, or tap Clear to empty the list.

Terrain height is not accounted for – always correct after the first round.
