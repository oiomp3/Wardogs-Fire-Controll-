Building better: tips for FOBs, bunkers and the MG Nest

5 minUpdated: October 2026

What structures cost and how to hack a FOB is covered in the FOB & base building guide. This one is about the how: building faster, walling up properly and making good use of the three new Season 2 buildables.

Placing the FOB and using free gear

  • Don’t just drop it anywhere. Put the FOB under a staircase or another roof. That keeps at least part of it safe from mortar hits.
  • Wall it in completely. If an enemy reaches the FOB, they can hack it. That pays them $2,500 and $1,000 to each teammate in range.
  • Take the tools from the FOB. The FOB contains a medium hammer, so you don’t need to buy one. There are wrenches for repairs too.
  • Check the towers. Towers can have loot on them, sometimes even a FOB. Otherwise it costs $7,500 at the Vendor.
  • Signal with smoke. A purple smoke grenade puts a landing marker (“H”) on the map, a green one a “supplies needed” icon. That attracts pilots who deliver crates and pallets.

Building faster: the yellow cross

Every structure has a critical spot, the yellow cross. Hitting it gives more build progress.

  • Since update 0.1.2 (September 30), the spot no longer jumps into corners or to the far side. It prefers a spot you can reach from where you are standing.
  • Since Season 2, the critical spot gives a smaller bonus than before. The medium hammer does less per swing, the large one builds as fast as before.
  • The cross appears as soon as you place something. On large structures, the spots sit close together.
  • Don’t chase it. Stay at maximum reach and rather take a step back. Then the cross tends to stay in one place.
  • Let go briefly. There is a short pause in the animation after each swing. If you release the left mouse button for a moment right then, you swing noticeably faster.
  • Lost the cross? Press V to dismantle. The cross moves as soon as progress goes above or below 25 % or 50 %. So a short dismantle makes it visible again.

Snapping, obstacles and adapting prefab structures

  • Turn off snapping. If a foundation turns red (“Foundation obstructed”), press B to turn off snapping and place the piece freely. The game then fills the gap to the obstacle with sandbags.
  • Since Season 2, foundations fit better against slopes, stairs and walls.
  • Adapt prefab structures. You can remove single parts of a finished structure without the whole thing disappearing. That way a large shelter becomes a garage for the spawn vehicle.
  • Don’t remove load-bearing parts. If you dismantle a load-bearing part, such as the wood of a bunker, the whole structure is gone. Since Season 2, unfinished structures collapse as soon as their support is gone.
  • Few doors. Every door can be left open. One door per structure is usually enough.

Walls: higher and heavier

Many bases only have one row of HESCO blocks. That isn’t enough: with a vehicle as a step you can get over it, and from roofs, church towers or hills a tank can see straight into the courtyard.

  • Build at least two layers high or use the large hammer to put concrete walls (Bremmer) on top of the HESCO row. Over a wall that high, you can barely see into the base from above.
  • Since Season 2, barbed wire and sandbags fit on top of Bremmer walls too.
  • The Vanguard CIWS shreds light cover but struggles against heavy fortifications. Another reason for concrete on the outer walls.

MG Nest (M2 Browning)

The MG Nest is a manned M2 Browning behind sandbags. Only the large hammer builds it. It costs 650 build supplies, and whoever destroys it gets $650.

  • 60 rounds per belt. A reload takes about 8 seconds and costs 200 of the FOB’s ammo supplies. So only reload when the belt is almost empty, ideally in cover.
  • Aiming down sights (ADS) makes it very accurate; from the hip it sprays a lot.
  • It traverses about 180°. You can barely aim upwards, but downwards works well. So build it high up.
  • An MG Nest takes about seven C4 to destroy.

Wooden Step: stairs and firing slits

Any hammer can build the wooden step. It is a quarter of a HESCO high, so four steps stacked make one full HESCO height.

  • You can stack at most five steps from the ground. For longer stairs, use single and double HESCOs as intermediate steps.
  • Firing slits: With steps you can leave an opening in a wall instead of having it filled with sandbags. This only works one step wide. Up to four steps high it stays open; from five, the game fills it with sandbags. From two steps high you can crawl through.

Large Shelter and a multi-storey bastion

The Large Shelter is available from the medium hammer up and costs 250 build supplies. It only withstands about four C4, so:

  • Put HESCOs or tank traps on the roof against mortars and protect the support pillars.
  • A second shelter snaps onto the first one. On a slope this takes some patience.
  • Bastion: an MG Nest facing forward on the ground floor, one per side on the first floor, and several MG Nests plus a Talon against helicopters on the roof. HESCO pillars in the middle of each level make sure a grenade or Molotov only hits part of the crew.
  • Build something like this high up or on open ground, not in a dip.

The main gate as a kill zone

Build a long walled corridor in front of the main gate so friendly vehicles can drive in and out safely. At its end goes a bunker or recon tower with an MG Nest aimed right down that corridor. With two MG Nests, one can reload while the other keeps firing.

  • Since Season 2, hammers no longer break open doors and gates. Only the Halligan Bar can do that ($885, 7 uses), and now it works on gates too.
  • Doors cost 80 and gates 200 build supplies.
  • Closing a door of your own FOB pays $75. There is a cooldown, and the payout shrinks over time.

Levelling Support faster while building

These structures earn the most cash and XP per hammer while you build up the FOB:

HammerStructure
SmallTank trap (hedgehog)
MediumLarge Shelter or loudspeaker tower (the cross is easier to find on the shelter)
LargeMG Nest (easy-to-see critical spots)

Only build what your base really needs. BULKHEAD punishes XP farming and exploits with resets and up to permanent bans. The progression planner shows which unlocks await you in the Support track.

💡 Tips

  • Build the wall before you build weapons. An MG Nest without cover won’t last long.
  • An MG Nest drains the FOB’s ammo supplies quickly. Plan your logistics accordingly.
  • Want to try a new building trick? Test it in the firing range first.
