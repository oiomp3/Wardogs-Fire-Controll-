🎯 Artillery

Countering artillery: taking out mortars and the SPH-2

1 minUpdated: October 2026

When your team is pinned down by artillery, falling back won’t help. The artillery has to go. BULKHEAD explicitly rewards that.

Contents 3
  1. Why countering pays off
  2. How to find the artillery
  3. Protection

Why countering pays off

  • A destroyed SPH-2 pays $8,000, an L81 mortar $650.
  • According to BULKHEAD, rewards for attack vehicles go up with their kills. So artillery that dominates your team is especially rewarding.
  • Since Season 2, mortars need more ammo supplies. Disrupt the enemy’s logistics and you cripple their artillery.

How to find the artillery

  • Listen for firing sounds and watch impact patterns. They tell you the rough direction.
  • Mortars are usually at or near a FOB because they need build supplies and ammo.
  • Use spotters with binoculars or a Range Finder and mark the position with a ping.

Once you know the position, our WARDOGS mortar calculator works out distance, bearing in mil and elevation for your own counter-strike with the L81.

Protection

  • Bunkers and air-raid shelters are buildable (200 build supplies).
  • The new Large Shelter (250) protects important assets.
  • Don’t stand around in tight groups.

Whether the Vanguard CIWS intercepts mortar rounds isn’t officially documented. Only its effect against aircraft and structures is confirmed.

💡 Tips

  • A fast vehicle with an RPG-7 or MAAWS is often the best artillery counter.
