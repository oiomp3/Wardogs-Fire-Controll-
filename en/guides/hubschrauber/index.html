Flying helicopters: MH-6, Little Bird, Lakota and Havoc

1 minUpdated: October 2026

Helicopters are the fastest transports and the most dangerous weapons in WARDOGS – and for beginners, the most expensive lesson. Here’s what you should start with.

The AH-6M was moved further back because, according to BULKHEAD, it was too strong for such an early unlock.

First flights

  • According to Polygon, the MH-6 is the best place to start. Practice in the firing range before you take passengers.
  • Flying passengers into the Hot Zone pays double (Hot Zone: double cash rate).
  • Flares help against missiles. According to the community, the default key is V.
  • Since Season 2, cockpit glass works both ways: if you shoot through it from the inside, you damage the helicopter.

Dangers

Since the October 2 hotfix, the Vanguard CIWS destroys a Havoc in about 6 instead of 12 seconds. On top of that there are the Flakpanzer Gepard, shoulder-fired Verbas and Talon emplacements (see Anti-air).

💡 Tips

  • Flying with a controller? BULKHEAD unified the damping of helicopter inputs across all devices.
  • Never hover low and slow over an enemy FOB.
