Loadouts by budget: cheap, mid-range, expensive

1 minUpdated: October 2026

In WARDOGS every life costs money. A good loadout is one you can afford more often than you die. Add ammo, bandages and a few small items on top – it’s best to check their prices directly at the Vendor.

Budget loadout (for your first hours)

  • AK74 ($800) plus ammo
  • Bandages
  • Optionally binoculars ($180)

Costs only a small part of your $10,000 starting cash. So you can die a lot without going broke.

Medic loadout (mid-range)

  • PP-19 Bizon ($1,000) or MP5 ($1,800, from Medic level 4)
  • Field Resuscitator ($425)
  • Medical Bag ($1,800, from Medic level 14)
  • Bandages, smoke grenade

With the Bizon, Field Resuscitator and Medical Bag you’re at about $3,225 plus ammo and consumables.

Assault with anti-tank (mid-range)

  • AK74 ($800)
  • RPG-7 ($1,800)
  • Level 3 armor and helmet ($4,500)

Around $7,100 plus ammo. Worth it if your squad hunts vehicles. A destroyed T-72B3, for example, pays $8,000.

Full armor (expensive)

Level 4 armor and helmet cost $9,500 together. Since Season 2 the Level 4 helmet muffles sound and proximity chat, restricts your field of view and fogs up when you sprint. Its damage reduction dropped from 65% to 60%. Mainly worth it for holding the zone and against suppressive fire: the Level 4 helmet reduces suppression by 80%.

💡 Tips

  • Do the math before you buy: how many lives can you afford with this loadout?
  • A team that is at least 40 points behind second place gets a 20% discount at the Vendor.
