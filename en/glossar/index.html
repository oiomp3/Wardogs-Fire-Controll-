Glossary

BCDEFGHIKLMPRSTVW
Bremmer wall
Buildable concrete wall. Since Season 2 you can put barbed wire and sandbags on top of it. → FOB & base building
Build supplies
Resource for building at a FOB. Since Season 2, for example, an L81 mortar costs 600 and a Vanguard CIWS 1,200 build supplies. → FOB & base building
CIWS (Vanguard)
Buildable close-in anti-air gun. Since October 2, 2026 it destroys a Havoc in about 6 seconds. → Anti-air
Control Zone (CZ)
The randomly chosen area (2 × 2 km) everyone fights over. Every 30 seconds, the team with the most players inside scores a point. → How to win
Delivery streak
Season 2 bonus for several logistics deliveries in a row. → Logistics & supply
Drill Rig
Buildable drilling rig that runs on fuel and affects the Hot Zone. Since Season 2 it runs for 300 seconds at most. → Towers, Control Zone and the Hot Zone Magnet
Elytra
WARDOGS’ kernel-level anti-cheat system. → Errors & crashes
FOB
Forward Operating Base – a forward outpost that stores supplies and lets you build. Costs $7,500 at the Vendor. → FOB & base building
Gold Bars
Permanent currency for cosmetics. Leftover cash is converted into Gold Bars at the end of a season. → Wipes & Season 2
Gold Market
Shop for cosmetic unlocks paid with Gold Bars. In Season 2 from WARDOG level 35. → XP & unlocks
Halligan Bar
Tool for breaching doors and gates (the only one since Season 2). $885. → FOB & base building
Hot Zone
A moving sub-area of the Control Zone. Players there count double and earn double money. → How to win
Hot Zone Magnet
Structure (2,750 build supplies) related to controlling the Hot Zone. The exact mechanics aren’t officially documented. → Towers, Control Zone and the Hot Zone Magnet
Infantry Mode
Community server mode without tanks, helicopters and heavy vehicles. Selectable in the deploy menu. → Community servers
Kolchia
The fictional, war-torn country where WARDOGS takes place. → The three factions
L81
Buildable mortar. Range according to community tables is about 132–684 m. → L81 Mortar & SPH-2
Large Shelter
Large metal shelter from Season 2 that covers key assets. Medium hammer and up, 250 build supplies. → Building better
MG Nest
Manned M2 Browning emplacement (Season 2). Large hammer only, 650 build supplies. → Building better
Mil
Angular unit for bearing and elevation. 6400 mil equal 360°. → L81 Mortar & SPH-2
Proximity chat
Voice chat that every player nearby can hear – enemies included. → Squad & voice chat
PV-1
Rare resource at the center of the backstory. → The three factions
Range Finder
Rangefinder for spotters. $1,450, the infrared version $3,200. → Spotting for artillery
Season / Wipe
A chapter of Early Access. When it changes, cash and XP are reset. → Wipes & Season 2
Spawn Vehicle (SV)
Vehicle your team can spawn on. Since Season 2 it’s tougher against the CIWS. → Vehicles & tanks
SPH-2
Self-propelled artillery, in Season 2 from Driver level 31. → L81 Mortar & SPH-2
Spotting
Scouting and marking enemies, vehicles or structures. Pays money based on the value of the target. → Spotting for artillery
Suppression
The suppressing effect of incoming fire. Level 3/4 helmets reduce it by 68 and 80 % respectively. → Ammo, armor & ballistics
Talon 9K-SAM
Buildable anti-air missile site (650 build supplies). → Anti-air
Tower
Capturable objective in the zone. According to community guides, towers are tied to moving the Hot Zone. → Towers, Control Zone and the Hot Zone Magnet
Track
Progression bar of a role: Assault, Medic, Recon, Support, Pilot, Driver and WARDOG. → Classes & roles
Vault
Storage for items you received, e.g. via Twitch Drops. → Codes & Twitch Drops
Vendor
The trader where you buy your gear for each life. → Loadouts by budget
Wager Vendor
Experimental bet on yourself (Season 2). You choose objectives and a stake and win big – or lose everything. → Making money
Wooden Step
Wooden step from Season 2, buildable with the small hammer and up. Good for stairs and firing slits. → Building better
